## Engineering result

Added a dependency-free `emp1-benchmark-comparison-custody/v1` owner that can retain a future actual external-comparison execution without becoming a WRC evaluator, tolerance owner, route-authority owner, or code/release authority.

The owner admits only completed `emp1-benchmark-evidence/v1` comparisons in one of:

```text
COMPARISON_QUALIFIED
WITHIN_TOLERANCE_NOT_QUALIFIED
OUTSIDE_TOLERANCE
```

`OUTSIDE_TOLERANCE` is intentionally retainable. Custody records truthful disagreement; it is not a PASS gate.

The owner requires:
- exact benchmark/comparator/case identity;
- frozen expected-value/tolerance anti-circularity evidence;
- explicit `ACTUAL_EMP_COMPARISON_EXECUTION` provenance;
- `DIRECT_EXECUTION_OBSERVATION` and `ACTUAL_EXECUTION_RESULT` source classification;
- exact repository commit, script path, execution timestamp and executor;
- observed quantity IDs matching the projected comparison quantity set/order;
- supplied route-authority snapshot matching the projected route relationship.

It rejects:
- `REFERENCE_NOT_AVAILABLE` as a retained comparison result;
- `executed=false`;
- inferred-without-execution evidence;
- approximate values;
- console-summary-derived values;
- manually reconstructed values;
- route-authority drift;
- authority leakage;
- unfrozen or production-derived expected/tolerance evidence.

Output is deeply immutable and all method/engineering/production/code/release authority flags remain false.
