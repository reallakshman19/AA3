6. CURRENT EXCLUSIONS / PENDING TECHNICAL WORK

Still explicitly not qualified:

A. Curved holes
- Holes on cylindrical midsurfaces remain blocked.
- Must combine multiply-connected UV topology, curvature sizing, material-ligament rules, seam-periodic topology where applicable, and physical 3D hole-boundary verification.
- Do not combine curved-hole qualification and multi-patch seam qualification in one change.

B. Multi-patch seams / continuity
- Separate surface patches require explicit interface ownership.
- Need deterministic common-edge parameter correspondence, conforming node identity, director/tangent-frame compatibility, manifold incidence, and no duplicate interface DOFs.
- Must distinguish C0 geometric continuity from any stronger kinematic/director continuity claim.

C. Additional analytic curved surfaces
- Cone should be a separate analytic-surface slice because metric and curvature vary with axial coordinate.
- Sphere should be separate because no single regular global rectangular chart exists; pole/chart singularities must be owned explicitly.
- Do not generalize the cylinder implementation by relabelling it as arbitrary curvature.

D. Offset-surface generation
- Not qualified.
- Requires declared reference-surface ownership, offset sign convention, normal field, thickness/midsurface semantics, self-intersection checks, and exact lineage from source geometry to generated midsurface.

E. Thickness-transition meshing
- Not qualified.
- Requires explicit thickness-field ownership and transition/refinement policy. Mesh sizing must not silently infer structural adequacy from thickness alone.

F. Shell local refinement
- Not qualified.
- Existing P2-9 retained refinement authority is LAFEA.3 T3/T6 only.
- Shell refinement needs target identity, surface reprojection, director reconstruction, seam/interface preservation, topology recertification, quality recertification, and atomic custody replacement.

G. NURBS / general freeform surfaces
- Last-stage scope.
- Requires an authoritative surface evaluator, derivatives/normal field, chart/trim topology, parameter-space metric control, projection/inversion rules, seam/singularity handling, and independent geometric-error qualification.
