7. REMAINING SHELL-MESH ROADMAP
-------------------------------

R1 — Curved holes
Status: NOT QUALIFIED.
Need a curved-surface hole topology contract, material-ligament metric on the surface, constrained parameter-space hole treatment, analytic-surface containment checks, physical facet/director checks, and workbench/evidence qualification. Do not inherit planar ligament rules without proving the surface metric and seam behavior.

R2 — Full-cylinder seam closure
Status: NOT QUALIFIED.
Current cylinder is deliberately non-wrapping. Full 360 degree shells require periodic parameter topology, deterministic seam-node identity/welding, no duplicate stiffness at the seam, canonical ordering independent of seam start angle, and solver-frame/director continuity evidence.

R3 — Multi-patch seams and continuity
Status: NOT QUALIFIED.
Need explicit patch adjacency, conforming shared-edge node authority, C0 geometry continuity checks, director/tangent compatibility, deterministic cross-patch node identity, non-manifold rejection, and evidence lineage that does not double-own seam entities.

R4 — Additional analytic curved surfaces
Status: NOT QUALIFIED.
Candidate sequence:
- cone/frustum;
- sphere/spherical patch;
- only after those are qualified, consider general parametric/NURBS/freeform surfaces.
Each surface class needs an exact or governed evaluation map, inverse/parameter custody where required, tangent/director basis, singularity handling, curvature sizing metric, and independent physical-facet qualification. Do not market this as generic curved meshing until the contract supports it.

R5 — Offset-surface generation
Status: NOT QUALIFIED.
Need declared reference-surface ownership, signed offset convention, thickness/midsurface relation, self-intersection detection, offset-boundary treatment, and proof that generated analysis geometry matches shell formulation assumptions. No inference from visual/render facets.

R6 — Thickness-transition meshing
Status: NOT QUALIFIED.
Need thickness field ownership, transition representation rules, element-property partitioning, grading/quality limits, solver/material handoff evidence, and convergence qualification around discontinuities.

R7 — Shell local refinement
Status: NOT QUALIFIED.
Current refinement authority is LAFEA.3 T3/T6 only. Shell refinement needs surface-aware insertion, exact parent mesh/artifact lineage, boundary/seam/hole preservation, curvature/director qualification after refinement, deterministic replay, and atomic custody replacement. Q8 and shell refinement must not be enabled by analogy.

R8 — General curved/freeform meshing
Status: FUTURE / HIGHER RISK.
Prerequisites should include robust parametric trimming, curvature/adaptive metric governance, singularity/trim handling, deterministic projection/inverse rules, patch topology, and an expanded numerical qualification ladder. This should be a separate authority programme rather than an incremental flag on the cylinder implementation.
