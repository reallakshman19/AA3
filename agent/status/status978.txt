3. QUALIFIED MECHANISM IS NOT YET FINAL ARCHITECTURE
======================================================================

The I015 selection:

    input.kind === 'FRAME' && teeModifier === null

is an experimental A/B boundary, not the permanent physical boundary.

Intended architecture:

    governed pipe section/material
      -> governed base beam constitutive law
      -> component-owned mechanics
      -> offsets/transforms
      -> assembly
      -> Schur condensation where required

Transverse shear belongs to flexible pipe beam mechanics. A tee does not make
adjacent pipe Euler-Bernoulli; a bend incoming straight is still straight pipe;
a reducer condenser does not remove shear flexibility.

Component mechanics must compose without double ownership. If a B31J junction
flexibility is represented by explicit spring/compliance authority, the same
flexibility must not be embedded again through a second weakening mechanism.

For condensed systems:

    Kc = Kbb - Kbi Kii^-1 Kib
    fc = fb  - Kbi Kii^-1 fi

Schur condensation is neutral: it preserves whatever stiffness/load mechanics
were assembled internally, whether correct or incorrect. Reducer internal
cylinders must therefore be qualified together with pressure/thermal load
ownership before a system cutover.
