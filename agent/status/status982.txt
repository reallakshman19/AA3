4. ROOT CAUSE FOUND DURING QUALIFICATION
----------------------------------------
The initial curved-hole candidate exposed a real deterministic lattice-phase
quality defect in the shared constrained-Delaunay interior seeding.

Old interior refinement revision:
  LAFEA.10.CDT-HOLES-TRI.V3
  BOUNDARY_CLEARANCE_FACTOR = 0.18

Observed before repair:
  - one-hole target 10 mm produced one quality BLOCK
  - scaled Jacobian was approximately 0.19719, below the unchanged 0.20 floor
  - the corresponding UV scaled Jacobian was approximately 0.1971605
  - the corresponding 3D scaled Jacobian was approximately 0.1971920
  - therefore cylindrical mapping was not the cause
  - the triangle was an outer-boundary sliver caused by an interior triangular
    lattice seed lying only about 2.08 mm inside a constrained boundary

The two-hole diagnostic ladder confirmed this was not safe to address by
cherry-picking a passing target. Under V3, accepted/blocking states varied with
target-size phase.

Rejected remedies:
  - relaxing the scaled-Jacobian threshold
  - hiding the BLOCK in evidence
  - post-generation uncontrolled quality repair
  - qualifying only selected lucky targets
