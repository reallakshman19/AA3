4. I026 — CAESAR METALLIC-PIPE SHEAR AUTHORITY — COMPLETE
======================================================================

I026 is authority-only. It changes no solver/profile mechanics and introduces
no numeric kappa.

Pinned vendor findings:
- Hexagon CAESAR II 14 documentation states that material shear modulus is
  required to develop a piping stiffness matrix in the orthotropic/FRP
  discussion.
- The explicit Ratio Shear Modulus: Elastic Modulus configuration located in
  the Version 14 documentation is FRP-specific.
- The ordinary isotropic material path documents elastic modulus and Poisson
  ratio; the retrieved vendor material does not expose an isotropic metallic
  straight-pipe effective shear area or shear-correction coefficient.
- Hexagon B31J documentation identifies tee flexibility as rotational correction
  mechanics; it does not provide authority for the base straight-pipe shear law.

Pinned primary mechanics source:
G. R. Cowper, "The Shear Coefficient in Timoshenko's Beam Theory," Journal of
Applied Mechanics, 33(2), 335-340, June 1966, DOI 10.1115/1.3625046. The paper
establishes a three-dimensional-elasticity basis for Timoshenko shear
coefficients, but it is not evidence that CAESAR II uses Cowper or the current
thin-annulus kappa=0.53 experiment.

Important negative-evidence rule:
absence of a metallic kappa from the searched Hexagon documentation is NOT proof
that CAESAR omits transverse shear. The implementation remains unresolved and
therefore fails closed.

I026 verdict:

    ACCEPTED_AUTHORITY_BOUNDARY_NO_NUMERIC_CANDIDATE_AUTHORIZED

Evidence record:
benchmarks/LFEA/CAESAR_ACCDB/m047/M047-I026.authority.json

Next admissible evidence must be one of:
1. Hexagon CAESAR II 14 theory/technical documentation containing the isotropic
   piping element stiffness equations and effective shear-area definition;
2. a Hexagon support/engineering statement identifying the metallic pipe
   transverse-shear implementation and shear-area/shear-coefficient rule; or
3. another vendor-authored implementation reference with explicit CAESAR II 14
   applicability.

Until then, do NOT try kappa=0.5, 5/6, another Cowper variant, a geometry-
dependent factor, or any coefficient selected by BM4_NL fit.
