- `NOT_REVIEWED`

- `REVIEW_REJECTED`
- `REVIEW_STALE`

A record shall bind at least:

- source hash;
- A result hash;
- B result hash;
- C result hash;

- retained route-authority hash, checked against the retained route-authority snapshot identity.
